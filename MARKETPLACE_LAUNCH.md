# Marketplace — 90 Day Launch Strategy

> **The wedge.** Marketplace is the lowest-friction entry point for the entire
> Contractor Flow ecosystem. Win Marketplace first → cross-sell Launchpad + CRM
> to those same customers later.

---

## 🎯 90-day goal (the bar to hit)

| Metric | Day 30 | Day 60 | Day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homeowner quote submissions / mo | 200 | 600 | 1,500 |
| Paying contractors ($49/mo) | 5 | 25 | 75 |
| MRR from Marketplace | $245 | $1,225 | $3,675 |
| Cold-outreach reply rate | 8% | 12% | 18% |
| Organic SEO traffic / mo | 0 | 500 | 3,000 |
| Email list size | 50 | 250 | 1,000 |

**Why these numbers:** 75 paying contractors × $49 = $3,675/mo MRR. That's
your "ramen profitable" line. Once you hit it, Launchpad upsells become
gravy on top.

## 🧩 Why Marketplace first (recap)

| Module | Friction | Speed to first $1 | Cross-sell power |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Marketplace** | $49/mo, universal pain (leads) | Days | Leads to all 3 modules |
| Launchpad | $1,997, trust + cash | Weeks | Already have customer |

Marketplace wins because:
1. Free homeowner conversion = volume
2. Universal pain (every contractor wants more leads)
3. $49 is the lowest paid threshold
4. Already deployed (no more building)

## 🛤 The customer journey

### Side A — Homeowner side (free, drives volume)

```
Cold visitor → /quote/[trade] → submit → SMS to matched contractor → quote sent → DEAL
                                       ↓
                                  Lead lands in your DB
                                       ↓
                              Contractor pays $49/mo for access
```

**Conversion target:** 3% of page visits → submitted quote.
**CAC target:** under $8 per lead (paid). $0 from organic.

### Side B — Contractor side ($49/mo, drives revenue)

```
Cold contractor → cold email/DM with personalized lead preview →
"$49/mo, first week free, here are 10 leads in your zip" →
they sign up → they close 1-2 leads → they stay
```

**Conversion target:** 10% reply rate, 30% of replies → first call, 50%
of calls → free trial, 60% of trial → paid.

## 📅 30-60-90 day roadmap

### Days 1-30 — Seed (your only job: prove the funnel works)

**Marketing:**
- $20/day Meta Lead Ads → `/quote/roofing`, `/quote/electrical`, `/quote/hvac`
- $0 Google Ads (wait until you have CTR data from Meta)
- Submit sitemap to Google Search Console + Bing
- 10 cold-DM 10 contractor friends to test the contractor side free
- Post in 5 MA Facebook groups (homeowner side: "free quotes")
- Daily IG/TikTok/LinkedIn post (one per day per platform, batch on Sunday)

**Product:**
- Don't add features
- Daily Supabase check for new leads
- Respond to every contractor reply within 1 hour

**Win condition by day 30:**
- 200 quote submissions
- 5 contractors trying it free
- 2 paying contractors ($98 MRR)

---

### Days 31-60 — Scale signal (double down on what worked)

**Marketing:**
- Bump Meta to $50/day if CAC under $10
- Launch Google Search Ads ($30/day) on keywords that converted in Meta
- Start cold-emailing 20 contractors/day from permit data
- Run 2 partnership pilots:
  - Home Depot Pro Desk in 1 MA store
  - 1 MA-based real estate agent referral deal ($25/lead)
- Add 3 trades to the homeowner quote flow if data shows demand
- Launch the founder podcast / YouTube interview circuit (5 episodes)

**Product:**
- Add "lead density by ZIP" page so contractors see proof before signing up
- Add weekly digest email to contractors ("here's what was in your zip this week")

**Win condition by day 60:**
- 600 quote submissions / mo
- 25 paying contractors ($1,225 MRR)
- 500 organic search visits / mo

---

### Days 61-90 — Compound (turn customers into your sales team)

**Marketing:**
- Referral program: $50 credit per contractor referral
- First case study: featured contractor + ROI story
- Launch a media kit. Pitch 5 Boston / MA publications
- Run 1 Launchpad upsell campaign to existing Marketplace contractors
- Hire a part-time VA ($300/mo) to do 50 cold outreach/day

**Product:**
- Add the Marketplace contractor dashboard (lead pull-down, claim, status)
- Add Stripe Customer Portal upgrade flow (Marketplace → Marketplace + Launchpad bundle)

**Win condition by day 90:**
- 1,500 quote submissions / mo
- 75 paying contractors ($3,675 MRR)
- 3 Launchpad sales originated from Marketplace customers ($5,991 + $1,491 MRR)
- 3,000 organic search visits / mo

## 📺 Channels — what each does (in priority order)

### 1. Meta Lead Ads (Facebook + Instagram)
**Job:** Cheap, fast homeowner leads to seed the marketplace.
**Budget:** $20-50/day to start.
**Why first:** Lowest CAC for home services. Lead form is in-app, no friction.
**Track:** CPL by trade, CPL by city.

### 2. Programmatic SEO (your /local/[service]/[city] pages)
**Job:** Long-tail free traffic. Compound over 60-90 days.
**Budget:** $0.
**Why second:** Already built and deployed. Now Google needs to index.
**Track:** Search Console impressions, top queries.

### 3. Cold outreach to contractors (manual + DM)
**Job:** Convert contractors to $49/mo subscribers.
**Budget:** Time. $300/mo VA after day 60.
**Why third:** Direct sales for the highest-revenue side of the marketplace.
**Track:** Reply rate, demo rate, signup rate.

### 4. Google Search Ads
**Job:** Capture high-intent "contractor near me" homeowner searches.
**Budget:** $30/day starting day 31.
**Why fourth:** More expensive than Meta but higher intent. Wait for Meta data.
**Track:** Conversion rate, cost per lead.

### 5. Local Service Ads (LSA — Google)
**Job:** Homeowners searching "roofer near me" see your listing at the top.
**Budget:** Pay-per-lead, ~$30-80/lead.
**Why fifth:** You're already applying. Will pay off Month 2-3 when approved.
**Track:** Cost per booked lead.

### 6. Organic social (IG, TikTok, LinkedIn)
**Job:** Brand awareness, founder story, free distribution.
**Budget:** Time only.
**Why sixth:** Slow but compounds. Critical for trust.
**Track:** Follower growth, story replies, DM volume.

### 7. Partnerships (Home Depot, real estate agents)
**Job:** Pre-warmed homeowner leads at low cost.
**Budget:** Revenue share or per-lead fees.
**Why seventh:** High effort to set up, high reward when it works. Pilot day 31+.
**Track:** Partner-attributed leads per month.

### 8. PR / press
**Job:** Trust signals, SEO backlinks.
**Budget:** Time + 1 PR push at day 60.
**Why eighth:** Compounds slowly, mostly indirect ROI.

### 9. Reddit, Nextdoor, Pinterest
**Job:** Niche organic reach.
**Budget:** Time only.
**Why ninth:** Hard to scale but free.

### 10. YouTube long-form
**Job:** Build authority over 6-12 months.
**Budget:** Time only.
**Why tenth:** Way too slow for the first 90 days. Pure brand bet.

## 🧪 Weekly experiment rhythm

Every Monday, pick 1 experiment. Run it for 5 days. Friday: kill or scale.

**Examples of weekly experiments:**
- Week 1: Test 3 IG ad creatives (homeowner side)
- Week 2: Test 3 cold-email subject lines (contractor side)
- Week 3: Test "free first week" vs "free first lead" offer
- Week 4: Test Facebook group post angles (educational vs promotional)
- Week 5: Test landing page hero (photo of home vs. video of contractor at work)

Document each in a `experiments.md` log. After 90 days, you'll have a
playbook of what works for YOU specifically.

## 🎯 Pricing strategy for Marketplace (be deliberate)

**Current price:** $49/mo, no contract, unlimited leads.

**Tactical moves:**

1. **First week free** for all new contractors. Anchors them. Use it as the
   close on every cold email.

2. **No-contract emphasis.** "Cancel anytime" beats "pay annually save 20%"
   for first-time signups. Lower the activation friction.

3. **Lead density floor.** *"If we don't deliver at least 1 qualified lead
   in your zip in 30 days, your next month is free."* Removes the "what
   if there are no leads?" objection.

4. **Tier up later.** Don't introduce a "Pro" or "Premium" tier yet. Get
   100 customers on the $49 plan first, THEN add a $99 tier with priority
   leads / exclusive zip codes.

## 🧱 What NOT to do in the first 90 days

- ❌ Don't build new features. Sell what's there.
- ❌ Don't add new trades unless data shows demand.
- ❌ Don't run TikTok ads. ROI takes 6+ months.
- ❌ Don't pitch national press. Focus MA only.
- ❌ Don't undercharge. $49 is the floor.
- ❌ Don't overcharge. Don't introduce Premium tiers yet.
- ❌ Don't ignore replies. Respond within 1 hour, always.
- ❌ Don't chase Launchpad sales until Month 2. Marketplace first.
