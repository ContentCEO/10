## TikTok (30 min)

Same handle, same profile pic. TikTok is **video-only** — you'll repost your
IG reels here. No carousels.

**Content strategy:**
- Post 3-4x/week
- Mostly 15-30s clips
- Format: hook in first 2s, value in middle, CTA at end
- 3 content pillars:
  1. **Marketing teardowns** — "I rebuilt this contractor's site in 30 seconds"
  2. **Build in public** — "Day 184 of building a SaaS in MA"
  3. **MA contractor culture** — "Why Mass contractors hate Angi" / "What a $1,997 contractor site actually looks like"

**First 5 TikToks:**

1. **"I built a free website preview tool for MA contractors"** — screen record + voiceover
2. **"Why most contractor websites are stuck in 2010"** — split-screen old vs new
3. **"Showing a real MA roofer his mockup site"** — emotional reaction style (you record yourself talking, show the preview URL on screen)
4. **"Three reasons I built Contractor Flow"** — talking head, 30s
5. **"The unfair advantage MA contractors are sleeping on"** — hook-heavy

**Hook formulas that work on TikTok (steal these):**
- "I'm a MA contractor and I just realized..."

- "Stop paying Angi $4k/month. Here's why."

# 🔁 BLOCK D — Cross-post automation (15 min)

You won't post on 12 platforms manually. Set up cross-posting.

**Option 1: Buffer** (free up to 3 channels, $6/mo for more)
- Connect: Instagram + TikTok + LinkedIn + Twitter + Facebook
- One post → 5 channels

**Option 2: Postiz** (open source, free, self-hosted on Vercel)
- More work to set up but $0/mo forever

**Recommended for today:** Buffer free tier. Connect IG + LinkedIn + Twitter. Move
to paid when you scale beyond 3 channels.

# 🎯 What "winning" looks like by day 30

- **1,000 IG followers** (organic + light boosting)
- **500 LinkedIn connections** (mostly MA contractor owners)
- **30 paying CRM customers** (~$1,500/mo MRR)
- **3 paying Launchpad clients** ($5,991 one-time or $1,491/mo MRR)
- **10 cold-outreach replies that turned into calls**
- **First testimonial from a real client**

If you hit half of these, you have product-market fit and you can raise a tiny
seed round (or just keep growing). If you hit none, the messaging needs work.
