# Content Bank — Marketplace launch + ongoing

> Copy-paste ready posts for the first 30 days. All written in CF brand voice.
> Cross-post the same idea to multiple platforms — adjust length per channel.

---

## How to use this doc

- **Daily**: pick 1 post, customize 1 detail (a town, a number, a name), publish
- **Sunday**: queue 7 posts in Buffer for the week
- **Mix angles**: don't post 7 days of "free quotes" — alternate homeowner-pitch / contractor-pitch / build-in-public / educational

---

## 🎯 Two audiences, two voices

| Audience | Tone | Hook style |
|---|---|---|
| **Homeowner** | Reassuring, easy, local | "Tired of [X]? Here's the fix" |
| **Contractor** | Direct, ROI-focused, peer | "Why your [X] is costing you" |

Don't mix on the same post. Pick one audience per post.
