# 90-Day Post Calendar

> Load this into Buffer in one sitting. After that, your social runs itself
> for 3 months.
>
> Each post has: **Date** · **Channels** · **Caption** · **Visual hint**.
> Most posts cross-post to 3-4 channels. Reuse the same caption across IG +
> LinkedIn + X with minor edits per platform.
>
> Visual prep: spend 1 hour in Canva creating 30 brand-template graphics
> (`brand/icon.svg` as logo, indigo/emerald/orange backgrounds, big serif
> text). Reuse them. Don't make 90 unique graphics.

---

## Cadence

- **Mon-Fri**: 1 main post/day, cross-posted
- **Sat**: Reel or TikTok (short video, no caption needed)
- **Sun**: Long-form LinkedIn carousel or thread

= ~7 posts per platform per week = ~90 days × 4 channels = 350+ scheduled.
Buffer free tier supports it.

## ⚙️ Buffer setup workflow (one-time, 90 min)

1. Open this doc on one screen, Buffer on another
2. For each post (90 of them):
   - Copy caption → paste into Buffer
   - Upload visual (or use brand template)
   - Set date + time
   - Add to queue
3. Bulk upload tip: Buffer has CSV import. Convert this doc to CSV
   (one row per post) and import all 90 at once. Saves 60 min.

4. Stagger times across day:
   - IG: 11 AM ET
   - LinkedIn: 9 AM ET
   - X: 7 AM ET and 4 PM ET
   - Facebook: 12 PM ET

5. Set up "queue paused if no recent posts approved" so a bug doesn't
   spam your audience.
