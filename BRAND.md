# Contractor Flow — Brand Foundation

> *"One lead. One contractor."*

This doc is the single source of truth for the Contractor Flow brand: what we
stand for, who we serve, how we sound, and how every sub-brand fits together.
Anyone — designer, copywriter, VA, future hire — should be able to read this
and write/design on brand without asking.

---

## 1. The 30-second pitch

**Contractor Flow** is the operating system for Massachusetts contractors.

Most contractor software is built by Silicon Valley for a generic "small business"
that lives in three time zones. Most contractor lead-gen is run by national
platforms that resell the same leads to five competitors. Both feel rented, not
owned.

We're different. We're built in Massachusetts, for Massachusetts. One ecosystem
with three modules:

- **CF CRM** — the dashboard that runs your day ($49-149/mo)
- **CF Marketplace** — real homeowner leads with name + phone ($49/mo)
- **CF Launchpad** — we build your site + run your ads ($1,997 or $497/mo)

Unlock what you need, ignore what you don't. Like ProPresenter for trades.

## 3. Brand values (what we won't compromise on)

1. **Local beats generic.** Every feature, every lead, every word is shaped
   for MA — towns, permits, weather, the way North Shore homeowners talk.
2. **Real beats theoretical.** A lead without a name and phone isn't a lead.
   A feature that doesn't ship in 2 weeks shouldn't be on the roadmap.
3. **Founder-led, not VC-led.** Davi answers the phone. Davi reads every
   support email. That's the moat.
4. **Plain English.** We don't say "leverage synergies." We say "we built it
   in 2 weeks." Contractors smell BS from a mile away.
5. **Own the work.** Customers own their leads, their site, their data. We
   don't lock you in. Leave anytime, take everything.

## 4. Audience personas

### Persona A — "Mike the Roofer"
- Owner-operator, 8-person crew, 12 years in business
- Marblehead, drives a Ram 2500
- Spends $4k/mo on Angi, gets ~30 leads, closes maybe 3
- Pain: "Half these leads are window-shoppers in California. I want real people in Salem."
- Reads: r/RoofingPros, Roofing Contractor magazine, Facebook MA trades groups
- Decision driver: ROI per lead. Trust in the salesperson.
- Selling to him: show him a mockup of his own site. Show him a real local
  homeowner lead, not a "qualified prospect."

### Persona B — "Jen the Homeowner"
- 34-year-old, just bought a 1920 Cape in Brookline
- Both her and her husband have full-time jobs, kids
- Needs 5 contractor projects (kitchen, bath, roof, paint, landscape) over
  the next 3 years
- Pain: "I want to call ONE place and have someone vetted call me back. I
  don't have time to vet 5 quotes."
- Reads: NextDoor, Houzz, Apartment Therapy
- Decision driver: trust, speed, transparency on price
- Selling to her: free quote in 60 seconds. Same-week callback. "Licensed +
  insured" prominent.

## 5. Sub-brand architecture

We use a **branded house** structure — Contractor Flow is the master brand,
each module is a sub-brand with its own color but always uses the CF parent
wordmark.

```
                    CONTRACTOR FLOW (parent)
                       indigo + violet
                              │
        ┌────────────┬────────┴────────┬───────────────┐
        │            │                 │               │
    CF CRM      CF MARKETPLACE     CF LAUNCHPAD    CF ACADEMY*
     indigo        emerald            orange          amber*
                                                   (*future)
```

**Rules:**
- Always lead with "Contractor Flow" or "CF" — never just "CRM" or "Launchpad"
  in marketing copy
- Sub-brand colors are accent only — the dark UI shell stays unified
- One favicon for everything (parent mark)
- Each sub-brand can have its own landing page section but never its own
  separate domain — `contractorflowstore.com/launchpad`, not `cflaunchpad.com`

## 6. Brand voice

**We sound like:** a smart friend who happens to know contracting and software.

**We don't sound like:** corporate SaaS, agency hype-bro, or condescending
to the trades.

### Voice principles

1. **Direct, no fluff.**
   - ✅ "We answer the phone when you call."
   - ❌ "Our customer success team is available during standard business hours."

2. **Confident but humble.**
   - ✅ "We're new. We're small. We're here."
   - ❌ "The industry-leading platform trusted by thousands."

3. **Specific over vague.**
   - ✅ "27 minutes to first contact. Average."
   - ❌ "Lightning-fast response times."

4. **Trades-respectful, not condescending.**
   - ✅ "You know how to swing a hammer. We handle the rest."
   - ❌ "Even non-technical contractors can use our platform."

5. **Local references freely.**
   - ✅ "Built for Mike in Marblehead, not Mike in Mountain View."
   - ❌ "Built for SMBs nationwide."

### Words we use
- Real, built, ship, leads, homeowners, MA, local, fast, ours, yours
- "We", "you" — never "the user", "the company", "our team"

### Words we never use
- Synergy, leverage, robust, scalable, enterprise-grade, best-in-class,
  cutting-edge, disrupt, revolutionize, ecosystem (*except* literally about
  CF's three-module setup), "solution"

## 10. Visual identity — quick reference

(Full visual specs in `brand/VISUAL.md`. This is the cheat sheet.)

**Logo:** Wordmark only — "Contractor Flow" in Instrument Serif, with the
"F" set in italic for the accent. Always paired with a gradient indigo→violet
icon block.

**Color palette:**

| Use                | Hex          | Tailwind class           |
|--------------------|--------------|--------------------------|
| Parent brand       | `#6366f1` indigo + `#8b5cf6` violet | `from-indigo-500 to-violet-500` |
| CF CRM accent      | `#6366f1` indigo                    | `bg-brand-500` |
| CF Marketplace     | `#10b981` emerald                   | `bg-emerald-500` |
| CF Launchpad       | `#f97316` orange                    | `bg-orange-500` |
| Background (dark)  | `#06060A` near-black                | `bg-[#06060A]` |
| Background (light) | `#f8fafc` stone-50                  | `bg-stone-50` |
| Text (dark theme)  | `#ffffff`                           | `text-white` |
| Muted text         | `rgba(255,255,255,0.6)`             | `text-white/60` |

**Typography:**
- Headlines: **Instrument Serif** (italic for accent words)
- Body + UI: **Geist Sans**
- Mono / labels: **Geist Mono**

**Photography style:**
- Wide-angle, ground-level, real homes (not stock office shots)
- New England architecture: capes, colonials, multi-family triple-deckers
- Natural light, working-day mood (not golden-hour real estate listing)
- People: hands on tools, not posed headshots
- Avoid: stock smiling guys in hard hats with crossed arms

## 12. What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't use stock photos of generic contractors with crossed arms
- ❌ Don't use words from the banned list (synergy, leverage, etc.)
- ❌ Don't bury the founder — Davi's name + face is the brand
- ❌ Don't rebrand a module — never "TradeCRM" or "FlowLeads", always
  "CF CRM" and "CF Marketplace"
- ❌ Don't use a fake testimonial. Use real ones with first name + town
- ❌ Don't say "we" if it's just you — "I built this in 2 weeks" beats
  "our team developed this solution" every time
- ❌ Don't pretend to be national. Lean into MA — it's the moat
